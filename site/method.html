SmogCast PKForecastEvaluationMethod

How it works

One gradient-boosting model per horizon, trained on hourly data with strictly past-only features.

1 · Data

134,496 hourly rows, 4 cities, Jan 2023 – Nov 2026. PM2.5/PM10 (CAMS) and weather from Open-Meteo.

2 · Features

39 past features: lags up to 1 week, rolling stats, trends, PM10, weather, hour/month, city. Plus 5 target-hour weather forecast features.

3 · Models

Point forecast, 80% conformal prediction interval and a smog-episode classifier (PM2.5 > 55.4) for horizons 1–24 h.

4 · Validation

Purged time-series CV, 4 baselines, ablation, day-block bootstrap, weather-noise stress test.

Limitations

  • PM2.5 comes from the CAMS model, not ground sensors.
  • Past-only forecasts beat persistence by only ~7% at 24 h; most of the gain needs weather forecasts.
  • The "80%" intervals actually covered 70–87% of outcomes across folds (under-covering in Dec/Jan winter, when volatility exceeds the autumn calibration window).
  • Real forecast-weather error was assumed (0.4σ), not measured.
